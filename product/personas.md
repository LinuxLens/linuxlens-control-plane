# Personas

## Platform / Linux engineer

**Goal:** know which hosts need work this week and what can be done without an outage.

**Needs:** host inventory, patch lag, reboot requirement, live-patch candidacy, concrete next actions.

**Does not want:** a CVE dump with no operational context.

## SRE / service owner

**Goal:** protect a critical workload (database, payments, identity) while reducing exposure.

**Needs:** reboot feasibility, maintenance-window impact, compensating controls, sequenced remediation.

## Head of infrastructure

**Goal:** a defensible estate-wide plan and investment case.

**Needs:** lifecycle runway, counts by risk band, migration vs extended-support trade-offs.

## CISO / risk leadership

**Goal:** explain residual Linux risk to audit, board, and regulators.

**Needs:** executive report, evidence of assessment method, what will change after the 90-day plan.

## Security engineer

**Goal:** connect vulnerability exposure to systems that cannot be patched conventionally.

**Needs:** kernel exposure, outstanding security updates, external exposure, finding traceability.

## Consultant / presales engineer

**Goal:** run a snapshot or deep assessment, including offline, and leave behind a report.

**Needs:** `linuxlens collect`, offline import, desktop/local analysis, executive + technical packs.

## MSP / partner operator

**Goal:** assess many customer estates with delegated access and consistent scoring.

**Needs:** organisation → workspace → estate tenancy, partner dashboard, white-labelled reporting.

## Compliance / GRC

**Goal:** evidence that unsupported systems are known, tracked, and planned.

**Needs:** lifecycle status, assessment history, exportable evidence, policy exceptions.
